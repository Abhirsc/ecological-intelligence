A small share of land, a huge share of life

2.4%of the world's land is India
7–8%of all recorded species live here
4global hotspots: Himalaya, Indo-Burma, Western Ghats, Sundaland
~75%of the world's wild tigers

Worldwide, about 1 million species face extinction. Rich places like India have the most to lose, and the most to gain from acting early.

Sources: MoEFCC (2018); Myers et al. (2000); Qureshi et al. (2023); IPBES (2019)
