Ecological intelligence is a loop

It turns what we observe in nature into timely, evidence-based decisions, then checks whether those decisions worked.

Why faster now? Satellites revisit every few days, AI labels millions of photos, and thousands of citizens report at once. Problems show up in months instead of decades.

Sources: Hansen et al. (2013); Norouzzadeh et al. (2018); Sullivan et al. (2009)
Evidence → action
1 · Observe
camera traps, satellites, apps
2 · Organise
open databases & maps
3 · Analyse
statistics, AI, models
4 · Act
protect, restore, alert, legislate
5 · Re-measure
did it work? loop again
