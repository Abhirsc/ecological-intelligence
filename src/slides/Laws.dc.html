50 years of laws for nature

1972Wildlife (Protection) ActProtected areas and species lists. Updated in 2022.
1976Articles 48A & 51A(g)Protecting nature becomes a duty of the State and of every citizen.
1980Forest (Conservation) ActCentral approval needed before forest land is used for anything else. Amended in 2023.
1986Environment (Protection) ActThe umbrella law. The EIA (Environmental Impact Assessment) rules of 2006 come under it.
2002Biological Diversity ActLocal committees and People's Biodiversity Registers. Amended in 2023.
2006Forest Rights ActRights for forest-dwelling communities and a voice for the gram sabha.
2010National Green TribunalA special court for environmental cases.
2017Wetlands RulesStates must map, notify and manage their wetlands.
