When field data reached the Supreme Court

~150Great Indian Bustards are estimated to be left in the wild
DataResearchers walked along power lines in the Thar and counted the birds killed in collisions. InsightHeavy bustards can't see the wires or turn away in time. ActionThe Supreme Court ordered bird diverters and underground lines. Its 2024 ruling also recognised a right to be free from the harms of climate change.
Sources: Uddin et al. (2021); M. K. Ranjitsinh v. Union of India (2021, 2024)
