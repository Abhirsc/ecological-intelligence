Eight pressures on India's nature

  1. 01Habitat loss & fragmentation. Roads, railways and mines cut through forests and wildlife corridors.
  2. 02Human–wildlife conflict. Elephants, leopards and people compete for shrinking space.
  3. 03Invasive species. Lantana and Prosopis juliflora crowd out native plants.
  4. 04Climate change. Heatwaves, erratic monsoons and coral bleaching.
  5. 05Dying wetlands, rivers & mangroves. Sewage, encroachment and sand mining.
  6. 06Grasslands & deserts labelled "wasteland". Open land is lost to plantations and projects.
  7. 07Insect & pollinator decline. Pesticides and lost flowers harm bees, and long-term insect data is scarce.
  8. 08Data gaps & silos. Many species are unrecorded, and existing data is hard to access.
Sources: IPBES (2019); Mungi et al. (2020); Sánchez-Bayo & Wyckhuys (2019)
