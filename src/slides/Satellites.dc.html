Green from space doesn't always mean healthy

24.62%of India was under forest and tree cover in 2021
DataLandsat maps tree cover change at 30 m detail. The Forest Survey of India reports every two years. InsightTree cover hides damage below the canopy: invasive Lantana has spread through more than 40% of the forests. ActionClear invasives and restore native plants where models predict the highest risk.
Sources: Hansen et al. (2013); Forest Survey of India (2021); Mungi et al. (2020)
